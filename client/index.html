              How to play

              Keyboard

              ↑↓←→ or WASD drive · Space handbrake · X use item · R reset · N restart race · Esc pause · M mute

              Gamepad

              Stick or D-pad steer · RT gas · LT brake · LB/RB drift · B item · Y reset · Back restart · Start pause

              Touch

              Steer with the pad on the left. GAS, BRAKE, DRIFT and ITEM sit on the right. ↺ resets the car and ⏸ pauses.

              Season

              Pick a circuit, the Classic Cup or the Space Cup, then a Buggy or a Monster Truck: everyone on the track drives the same one. Reverse drives every track the other way round, and Easy mode starts you 2nd and makes the opponents a little slower. Each has its own records. Finish in the top three to go through to the next round. Prize money buys upgrades in the garage between races. You get 2 continues to retry a race you miss.
